## 👋 About me

I'm a final year Computing Science student at the **University of Alberta**, specializing in **AI**, with over 30 months of experience. I like the problems people have learned to live with. I listen first, build around how people already work, and judge the result by one test: whether someone's day got easier.

I've done that at three very different scales: a consulting practice of fewer than five people, the **City of Edmonton**, and **Citi**.
